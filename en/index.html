About

Mohammed Ismael Yousif is an Iraqi visual artist and sculptor. His practice spans sculpture and painting: monuments and murals at the University of Anbar, iron line sculptures that turn verses by Nizar Qabbani into drawings in space, a long series of doves as symbols of peace and freedom, and paintings of Arabian horses in earthy, textured colour.

  • Diploma, Institute of Fine Arts, Baghdad
  • Bachelor's degree in Plastic Arts, Baghdad
  • Member of the Iraqi Artists Syndicate (General Centre)
  • Member of the Iraqi Plastic Artists Society (General Centre)

Sculpture

Iron sculptures, 60 cm high, each inspired by a poem by Nizar Qabbani, together with the dove sculptures.

The Doves series

A series in which the dove returns again and again, its wings shaped like open hands, in pastel on paper and in sculpture.
